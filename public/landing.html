Funded Membership (16–25)

People's Postcode Lottery is funding a free one-year membership for people aged 16 to 25. Verify your age below to claim yours.

How it works

  • Enter your name and email below.
  • A verification window from Veriff, a trusted identity-verification provider, will open so you can scan a photo ID (passport, driving licence or ID card).
  • If you're verified as 16–25, your free membership is set up automatically and you'll receive a sign-in email.

Privacy

We only receive your name, your email address, and whether the age check passed. We never see or store your ID document or your date of birth. Verification is carried out by Veriff under their privacy policy.

You must be aged 16–25 on the day you apply. One funded membership per person. Your free membership lasts 12 months from the day it's granted.

Funded by players of People's Postcode Lottery.
